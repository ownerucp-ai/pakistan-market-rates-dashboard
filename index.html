Gold & Bullion Pricing

Bullion rates in Pakistan are regulated by the All-Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association (APSGJA). Prices fluctuate based on international bullion markets and local currency exchange valuations.

Petroleum Price Mechanism

Petroleum prices are determined fortnightly by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) in alignment with global Brent crude benchmarks and Federal Petroleum Levy adjustments.

Poultry Market Index

Daily chicken meat and farm egg prices are issued by local district administration committees and the Pakistan Poultry Association (PPA) based on supply-demand dynamics.
